Who WE ARE

K.I.N.G. Renovation & Construction is a small, family-owned renovation and construction business built on years of experience in the residential construction field. Over the years, we have worked across different areas of the industry, gaining hands-on experience and developing a strong understanding of how a quality project should be planned, built, and finished.
As a family business, we believe in putting our name behind our work. We take pride in being involved in every project, paying attention to the details, and treating each home with the same care and respect we would give our own.
Our experience gives us a solid foundation, but we also believe that renovation is always evolving. We bring **fresh ideas, modern approaches, and practical solutions** to every project, combining what we've learned over the years with new ways of creating better spaces.
Whether it's a small renovation or a complete transformation, our goal is simple: **do quality work, build lasting relationships, and create spaces that our clients are proud to call home.
